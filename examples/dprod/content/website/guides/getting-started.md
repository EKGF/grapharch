# Start with a product people can identify

Begin with a concrete product and the people who need to understand it.
Describe its purpose, identify its owner, and distinguish the product from the
datasets and services through which it is delivered.

## A practical conversation

1. What does this product help someone do?
2. Who owns its description and lifecycle decisions?
3. Which datasets does it use or produce?
4. Which services or distributions provide access?

These questions introduce DPROD concepts; the authoritative term definitions and
SHACL constraints belong in the specification. A guide can explain a workflow
without reproducing every term or becoming another source of normative rules.
