# Make data products understandable

DPROD gives teams a vocabulary for describing a data product: its purpose,
owner, lifecycle, datasets, and ways to access it. A shared description helps
people understand what a product offers before they integrate with it.

## Explore the ideas

[Start with a product people can identify](guides/getting-started.md), then read
[the story behind a shared description](stories.md).

This companion website introduces the ideas and explains how to use them.
The specification defines the terms and constraints; the PDF provides a document
edition for review. Those publications have their own content selection and
arrangement. This showcase is a derivative preview, not an official OMG publication.
