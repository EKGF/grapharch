# GraphArch

Documentation from the sources you already maintain.

![Ontologies, taxonomies, use cases, datasets, and shapes flow through Collectors into the GraphArch Knowledge Graph. Renderers produce websites, standards, and PDFs.](assets/publication-flow.svg)

[Get started](usage/getting-started.md) · [Explore the gallery](showcases.md)

## Build your publication

- [Collectors](collectors/README.md): connect your sources.
- [Renderers](renderers/README.md): choose a format and layout.
- [Ontology specification](spec/develop/index.html): configure your publication in RDF.
- [Contribute](other/contribute/README.md): add a collector, renderer, or improvement.

GraphArch is an open-source [EKGF](https://ekgf.org) project,
[developed on GitHub](https://github.com/EKGF/grapharch).
